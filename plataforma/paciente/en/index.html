Permission to use your data

What we will keep
The photos of what you show (mole, spot, lump, sore or nail). Your personal data: name, date of birth, SUS health card and CPF number. Your answers about the lesion and about your health.

What it is for
So that a dermatologist can look at your lesion remotely and say whether you need further care. They may ask for a better photo, a test, or a biopsy (removing a tiny piece of the lesion for testing).

Who will be able to see it
The team at your city’s health center, the nurse in charge of the program, and the doctor who will assess your case. No one else. All of them are required by law to keep it confidential.

Where it is stored
In your municipality’s Health Department system, protected, for as long as the law requires medical records to be kept. Your data is not sold and does not go to companies that are not part of your care.

Your rights
You can ask to see your data at any time. You can ask to correct anything wrong. You can withdraw this permission later — just tell the health center. And you can refuse and still be cared for by the SUS as usual.

Important
This program does not give a diagnosis through the app. It organizes your information and takes it to the doctor. The doctor always decides.

Brazilian General Data Protection Law (LGPD) — Law 13.709/2018.
